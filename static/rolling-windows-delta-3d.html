Rebalancing Delta 3D Explorer

The rebalancing delta lifted into 3D: each window’s (ΔVol, ΔCAGR) pair placed at its end date. Use Down Z to look down the time axis and reproduce the 2D delta scatter.

Never rebalancing makes the delta identically zero — select Annually or Quarterly to see the strand. Drag to rotate; color encodes window end date by default, or switch to Quadrant to color by outcome. Use Focus to highlight a single quadrant — focused lines are thickened where they stay within that quadrant.
